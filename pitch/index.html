02 / The Business Problem

The control room sees the same settings. The furnace does not have the same internal state.

Blast volume, oxygen, temperature, and top pressure can look unchanged while gas distribution, cohesive-zone geometry, burden fines, alkali circulation, or hearth drainage quietly deteriorate.

What the sheet shows

Blast, oxygen, pressure, and ore/coke ratio remain near yesterday's values.

to
What the furnace hides

Wall gas channelling, higher pressure resistance, weaker coke, or an alkali/zinc circulation loop.

to
What the plant experiences

Lower tonnes, unstable silicon, higher fuel rate, and repeated wind cuts without a clear cause.

Hourly averages hide events

Slips, hangs, pressure spikes, and tuyere events occur below the one-hour sampling interval.

Inputs act on different clocks

Blast acts in 1 hour; PCI in 2.5; charged coke in 6.5; alkali accumulation over days.

Operator feedback confounds ML

A naive model learns how operators react, not necessarily what causes production to change.

07 / Machine Learning You Can Defend

A model only ships when it beats the simplest honest baseline.

In plain English: Last cast silicon is hard to beat because the furnace changes slowly. If ML cannot beat “use the last cast again,” it does not ship.

Why random splitting is banned

Adjacent hours share most of their state. Hearthline uses contiguous walk-forward folds with a 24-cast embargo.

Why forecast and decision models differ

A forecast may use any information known at prediction time. A setpoint recommendation may use causal parents only.

11 / Patentability and Intellectual Property

      
Can this use case be patented? Yes, with carefully scoped claims.

      
Textbook RAFT, Rist, and Ergun equations are not patentable. The stronger invention is the specific industrial workflow that combines target-conditioned Golden Ranges, physical lag convolution, sensor-anchored simulation, causal setpoint gating, and automatic MoC generation.

      

        
Four candidate inventions
  1. Lag-convolved hidden-state attribution when setpoints look unchanged.
  2. Sensor-anchored hybrid twin with derivative-preserving soft saturation.
  3. Confounding-aware optimizer inside a Mahalanobis envelope with five-ceiling arbitration.
  4. Evidence classifier that withholds unsafe absolute values but keeps response deltas.
